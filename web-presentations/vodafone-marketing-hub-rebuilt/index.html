How the hub works today.

Most of the work is manual, and the pages are hard to change.

01Finding content

Search matches titles, tags and descriptions, so results depend on how well each asset was tagged and described. Some searches find nothing.

02Adding assets

Every asset needs a description, tags and a thumbnail, all added manually, one asset at a time.

03Building pages

Every page is a set of boxes in a long scroll, with flags and stock photos as covers. Every portfolio page looks the same, and pages across several portfolios aren't possible.

04Updating the site

The page builder is fixed, and layout changes need development help. Help and Advice is static pages and PDFs.

Approach

How we'll do it.

8 to 10 weeks from start to switch-over.

  1. Understand today: the process, stakeholders, user journeys and gaps, for the central team and the markets.
  2. Interview one person from each team about what they need. The same people test it later.
  3. Design the UX, UI, features and content hub templates, and validate them with you.
  4. Move all current assets across, tagged in your taxonomy.
  5. Test with the central team, then with selected markets, and refine.
  6. Run the new hub alongside the current one on a new domain, and switch when everything works.

Next steps.

  1. Agree the scope and the content hub templates.
  2. Confirm the support, development and training included.
  3. Confirm the start date.
  4. Book the team and market interviews.
