What customers can do in the showroom

01

Choose a clinical space

Open a therapy gym or treatment room. Follow a suggested route or go directly to a product.

02

Choose the products on display

Show or hide equipment to focus on a selected combination. Find the same product in other relevant rooms.

03

Understand each product

Select equipment to see what it does, its main features and how it is used. Zoom or rotate supported 3D models.

04

Watch and read more

Play a demonstration, view an animation or open a brochure beside the relevant product.

05

Save products of interest

Build a shortlist and take away a summary with product information and resource links.

06

Discuss their facility

Request a demonstration or contact Bioness with selected products and details about their clinical space.
